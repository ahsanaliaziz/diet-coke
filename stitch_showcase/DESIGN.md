## Brand & Style
This design system embodies an editorial, high-fashion reinterpretation of iconic beverage culture. Merging the Pinterest moodboard sensibility with glossy editorial design, the aesthetic balances iced condensation, polished aluminum sheens, and an uncompromising noir backdrop. The brand personality is unapologetically chic, poised, confident, and crisp—evoking the sensory sensation of opening an ice-cold can at golden hour or inside a dimly lit atelier.

Drawing heavily from modern magazine layout structures and tactile glassmorphism, elements float like curated photography prints and gallery placards. Frosted surfaces mimic condensation on cold aluminum, while delicate hairline borders in brushed chrome establish quiet luxury. Crisp crimson highlights punctuate the experience with razor-sharp intent, serving as the electric pulse within a deep monochromatic field.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high-contrast drama between the pitch-noir canvas and shimmering brushed silver metals, electrified by the signature crimson spark:

- **Primary (`#E31837`)**: The classic crimson red accent. Used sparingly and with extreme intention—reserved for critical CTAs, active indicators, pull quotes, and statement badges.
- **Secondary (`#D1D5DB`)**: Frosted aluminum and light brushed chrome. Acts as the primary luminance channel for headlines, illuminated glass borders, and reflective surface highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#8A909A`)**: Muted metallic platinum. Deployed for secondary prose, metadata timestamps, subtle grid divider lines, and card subtitles.
- **Neutral (`#0F0F11`)**: Deep obsidian-noir. The absolute foundational canvas, delivering an immersive, high-end gallery atmosphere that allows cold drink photography and reflective metallics to pop.

Translucent glass layers are derived from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` backed by `16px` to `24px` backdrop blurs.

## Typography
Typography is treated like an haute-couture fashion publication. **Bodoni Moda** anchors display and headline positions with dramatic vertical contrast, razor thin serifs, and high-fashion authority. Headlines frequently employ italic variations for emphasized emotional words (e.g., *"Crisp,"* *"Silver,"* *"Iconic"*).

**Hanken Grotesk** offsets the expressive serif with clinical Swiss discipline. Used across body copy, navigational anchors, and technical lifestyle callouts, it maintains immaculate legibility against dark translucent glass and dark imagery. Labels and category pills use uppercase tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) to mirror archival print cataloging.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing into an organic, scrollable moodboard stack on mobile. Sections embrace generous vertical breathing room, rejecting cluttered eCommerce norms in favor of spacious editorial composition.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with dynamic column spans (e.g., 7-column oversized hero imagery intersecting 5-column glass floating cards). Outer canvas margins sit at a luxurious `4rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with unified `2rem` margins, enabling 2-column masonry moodboard grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with tight `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Full-bleed editorial card visuals alternate with offset micro-glass panels.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed via tactile glassmorphism and condensation-inspired atmospheric layering rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Base Canvas**: Solid `#0F0F11` with occasional ambient radial glows of crimson `#E3183715` (10% opacity, 120px blur) reflecting behind ice and can hero elements.
- **Glassmorphic Paneling (Level 1)**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed with a 1px continuous border gradient running from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` at the top edge to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` at the bottom edge.
- **Floating Modals & Interactive Flyouts (Level 2)**: Background `rgba(20, 20, 24, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered with `rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.25)`, elevated by an ambient shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Metallic Edge Reflection**: Selected primary components sport a delicate top-lit chrome bevel via `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.

## Shapes
The shape language favors a sleek, tailored, and architectural profile. With roundedness level `1`, the interface avoids bubbly curves, embracing subtle softened corners that feel modern, precise, and luxury-oriented.

- Standard interactive controls and input fields: `0.25rem` (4px).
- Editorial cards, image frames, and moodboard items: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Badges, status markers, and micro-capsule tags: `0.75rem` (12px) or full pill where specific tactile thumb loops are required.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Crimson (`#E31837`) fill with pure white label typography in uppercase tracked `label-lg`. Subtle inset highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`. Hover state triggers a subtle metallic crimson gloss shift and slight 1.02x scale.
- **Secondary Action**: Frosted aluminum ghost button. Translucent glass fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border 1px `rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.25)`, text in `#D1D5DB`. Hover fills with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` with silver border glow.
- **Icon / Minimalist Action**: Borderless square or circular tap target with `#D1D5DB` iconography that flashes `#E31837` on interaction.

### Moodboard & Editorial Cards
- Translucent container with `0.5rem` radius, 1px chrome border gradient, containing high-contrast photography of iced cans, droplets, and lifestyle vignettes.
- Metadata overlays feature Bodoni Moda titles with pinned capsule tags in the top-right corner.

### Chips & Badges
- Ultra-slim glass capsules (`0.75rem` radius), uppercase `label-sm` tracking, hairline silver borders `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Active filter chips switch to a solid crimson fill with obsidian text.

### Input Fields
- Deep obsidian fill `#141417` with a low-profile silver hairline border `rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#8A909A`. Focus activates a 1px `#E31837` stroke accompanied by a soft `0 0 12px rgba(227, 24, 55, 0.2)` aura.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square/circle metallic boxes with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` backgrounds and brushed borders. Checked state snaps into vivid `#E31837` with a crisp `#FFFFFF` micro checkmark or pip.

### Lists & Archival Tables
- Frameless, separated by delicate hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Items feature generous vertical padding (`space-md`), alternating between sans-serif metadata and Bodoni Moda titles.
